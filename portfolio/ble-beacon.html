Project Overview

Smart Bluetooth Low Energy Hardware Platform

The BLE Beacon Development Board was developed to deliver a compact, energy-efficient wireless solution for industrial asset tracking, smart monitoring, and IoT applications. The design focuses on reliable BLE communication, optimized power consumption, and a compact PCB layout.

Client Requirement

Develop a compact Bluetooth Low Energy hardware platform with long battery life, reliable wireless communication, and industrial-grade PCB design suitable for IoT applications.

Our Solution

360Electronix designed the complete embedded hardware, including schematic design, component selection, PCB layout, RF optimization, prototype development, and hardware validation to achieve a production-ready solution.
